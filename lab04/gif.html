GIF Format Example

About This Image

This image shows Goku and vegeta from the Dragon Ball sparking zero video game with blue hair as they prepares to use powerful energy attacks. The background is filled with bright blue energy effects that make the scene look intense and action packed.

Why gif Format?

GIF is a good format for this image because it supports animation, allowing Goku's movements and energy effects to be shown. This makes it useful for displaying short animated scenes on websites.
