PNG Format Example

About This Image

This image shows a red and black PlayStation 5 controller resting on a dark surface. The controller has two analog sticks, a directional pad, and several buttons.

Why PNG Format?

PNG is a good format for this image because it keeps the details of the PlayStation controller clear and sharp. This format is also useful for screenshots.
